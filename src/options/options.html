Preferences

Reading records are stored on this device and, when sync is enabled, on your configured server. After you consent, opened pages are sent to your model provider for article detection (except pages on your reading blocklist). Text translation sends the selected text and its surrounding paragraph to your model provider. It starts automatically only on sites in your translation allowlist; enable it manually from the popup elsewhere.

Before you start: sharing content with your model provider

Lectern uses your own API key to connect directly to the provider selected below. After you consent, it sends:

  • Automatically: The URL, title and page text (up to about 30,000 characters) of opened pages for article detection. Sites on your reading blocklist are excluded.
  • When you use a feature: Text and screenshot translation, explanations, follow-up questions, vocabulary chat, article review, AI filtering and rule editing send the text involved in that action: selected text and its paragraph, chat messages and recent conversation, article titles or text, or rules and the current page URL.

These requests go directly to your provider, whose privacy policy applies. Lectern sends nothing to a model provider before you consent. Privacy policy

Device sync

Connect to your server to merge reading history, translations and review data across your phone and computers.

The first sync uploads existing local records and downloads your account data. You can keep using Lectern offline.

Reading and translation preferences sync with your account. Model API keys stay on this device, and sync tokens are excluded from exports.

When sync is enabled, daily button counts are also sent to your server so its administrator can assess feature usage. These contain counts only; see Diagnostics.

Completion rules

An article is finished when both conditions are met: the read percentage reaches the threshold below, and the final paragraph has entered the viewport. This avoids treating partial reading or scrolling straight to the bottom as completion.

You can also change completion status in the workspace.
Resume reading

Remember the last paragraph read in each article and return to it when you reopen the page. Restore only when the page has not already set a position: a link with an #anchor, a position restored by the browser, or scrolling while the article is being extracted takes priority.

A notice and a Back to top button appear after restoring your position.
Article review

When you reach the completion threshold, Lectern saves the article text and asks your model for an outline and three recall questions. Pages you only glance at are not saved.

Generates review material automatically and uses model tokens.
Focus timeouts

Reading does not require input. A screen of 400 words takes about 100 seconds at 238 words per minute, so both timeouts adapt to the amount of visible text. The inactivity limit is the greater of the fixed timeout and 1.5 times the estimated screen reading time, capped by the adaptive maximum. The fixed value still applies when little text is visible.

Paragraph progress

A paragraph counts as read after it stays visible for a proportion of its estimated reading time. The default is 50%, which helps distinguish reading from quickly scrolling past.

Session rules

A focus session ends when activity signals stop. Switching tabs or reading a screen quietly can split continuous reading into several sessions. Reading rounds combine nearby sessions on the same article. The overview uses rounds and shows the merge threshold alongside them.

Combine sessions on the same article when their gap is no greater than this value.
Edit rules in plain language

Describe a change, such as “Stop tracking example.com” or “Automatically enable translation on GitHub.” Your model proposes a list of changes. Changes take effect only after you confirm. Invalid or overly broad rules are rejected. Each added rule shows how many existing reading records it matches.

Reading blocklist

One domain or full URL per line. Domains include subdomains; URLs include child paths. This does not affect translation.

Translation allowlist

Use the same rule format. Translation starts automatically on matching pages. On other pages, enable it from the popup for the current visit, or choose Always enable on this site. This does not affect reading records. The allowlist stays on this device and does not sync.

Data

Export JSON to back up your records, merge them on another device, or analyze them with other tools.

Clearing this device disconnects sync while keeping settings and API keys. Server data is not deleted. Imports merge articles, sessions, translations and review cards without duplicates. Cards for the same word are combined using the most recently reviewed schedule. Settings and API keys are unaffected.

Diagnostics

Lectern keeps the latest 10 model failures locally, including error details, raw responses and selected text. It also keeps timing information for the latest 50 calls, including successful calls. The app also keeps the latest 30 reader fetch logs and 30 runtime errors. These logs exclude API keys and are not included in data exports. When sync is enabled, diagnostic logs are also uploaded to your configured server.

Daily workspace button counts are kept for 90 days. They contain counts only, without article identities or search terms, and help assess which controls are useful.When sync is enabled, these counts are sent to your configured server for its administrator to analyze. Otherwise they stay on this device. Clear logs does not remove these counts.
